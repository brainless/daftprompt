## The motivating failure is in this folder

`b1` emitted `daftprompt (Tool) uses akar (Tool)` citing only the **second sentence** of
AGENTS.md line 7. Rejected twice over: seed `uses` is `Command→Tool` (subject kind
mismatch) and the partial quote fails strict whole-line matching. The same double
rejection hit `daftprompt uses gitoxide`, `uses SQLite FTS5`, `uses sqlite-vec`, and
`akar uses wgpu/glyphon/taffy`. Result: the application `daftprompt` — the subject of
R1, R2, R3 — appears in **no valid claim at all**. Also recorded: `graph build/search
requires XIAOMI_API_KEY` (shape-valid under this seed!) rejected **only** for quoting
L45-46 without line 44's `Before` clause. Epic 014's schema repair (add `Application`,
`uses(Application, Tool)`) plus snapshot-derived citations must fix exactly this.

## Entity-level observations

- No exact duplicate claims (unlike budget 150 in the stale report).
- The CLI family proliferates as distinct subjects (`cargo run`, `cargo run -- --repo .
  --index`, `…--index-code`, …) — invocation strings, not canonical entities.
- `daftprompt` is typed `Tool` in every emission that includes it — and every one of
  those was rejected, so the graph silently lost its central node.
- Minted vocabulary was healthy (`is_part_of`, `is_indexed_by`, `holds_vectors_for`,
  `tracks`, `owns`) and carried 24 of the 40 valid claims — but the minted `owns(File→Tool)`
  shape is what let the two L133 non-sequiturs through.

## What this baseline implies for Epic 014 scoring

A GLiNER2.5 run must beat, at minimum: 0/9 R-recovery, 20% misleading rate, and the
total loss of application-level subjects — while holding citation validity at 100% and
Q5/Q6 at correct absence. The repair levers it exercises are precisely the ones this
folder proves matter: endpoint-typing (`Application`), multi-pair predicates
(`uses(Application, Tool)`), snapshot-derived (not model-quoted) citations, and
entailment review that would have flagged the L133 `owns` claims and the L81/L25
wrong-action claims even though their citations matched exactly.
